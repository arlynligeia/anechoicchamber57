ANECHOIC CHAMBER 57 is a project that fuses film and fashion as part of a single narrative built in parallel. While the film's story was being written, the collection was being designed, and while the collection was being designed, the story was being enriched.

Likewise, the project explores a journey toward authenticity. Faced with a landscape dominated by minimalism and restraint, it proposes a work charged with color, shape, and character.

The collection's debut will be presented in the form of a medium-length film and a lookbook.

RELEASE

Lookbook: February 9, 2027

Medium-length film: 2027 (Date yet to be confirmed)
